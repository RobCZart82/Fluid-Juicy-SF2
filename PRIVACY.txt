Fluid Juicy SF2 privacy notice

Fluid Juicy SF2 reads the SoundFont file you select and processes MIDI and
audio locally. The plugin and standalone application do not send telemetry
or upload your SoundFont files.

To restore a session, Fluid Juicy SF2 saves the SoundFont's local path,
instrument settings and editor size in its state. On macOS the state can
also contain a file-access bookmark. A DAW such as REAPER may store this
state in a project or preset. The state contains a reference to the file
rather than its audio samples. When you share a project or preset, that
reference may reveal folder names or your local account name.

This notice covers Fluid Juicy SF2 itself. Your DAW, operating system and
any services you use to store or share projects have their own policies.


When you use Add folder in the Banks menu, Fluid Juicy SF2 stores that
folder's local path in your user application-data settings. On macOS it also
stores a file-access bookmark. Remove folder removes the saved reference.
Folder discovery reads filenames locally when you open or refresh the menu.
These library preferences are separate from the state saved in a DAW project.
